## 3. The "Day 1 to Mastery" Quality Doctrine

> *"In the beginning, we will make mistakes. But every single frame will be our absolute best."*

We recognize that early episodes will have imperfections. But we reject laziness, generic templates, and low-effort outputs. To ensure continuous quality ascension, **ORBIT must run this strict Quality Gate on every scene**:

### The 5-Point Quality Gate:
1. **The Specificity Test**: Could this scene have been described in a generic lecture? If yes, **fail**. Replace abstract talk with concrete visual physics (e.g. don't just say *"the server crashed"*; show 10,000 packets colliding at a bottleneck until the buffer overflows into a cascade of red telemetry).
2. **The Motion Physics Test**: Is anything moving linearly? If yes, **fail**. All motion must have weight, inertia, and spring tension (Remotion `spring()`, mass, damping, or exponential Bézier curves `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
3. **The Color Discipline Test**: Are there arbitrary hex codes? If yes, **fail**. Enforce Sanzo Wada harmony and our official network palette: **Dark Slate Gray (`#0B493A`)**, **Khaki Gold (`#F3B659`)**, and **Accent Orange (`#E65A28`)**.
4. **The Visual Hierarchy Test**: Can the eye immediately find the focal point in under 200ms? If the screen is a cluttered wall of unreadable nodes, strip away 40% of the noise.
5. **The Emotional Pacing Test**: Are we pausing for awe? A video cannot be 100% relentless speed. Build rhythm: **Tension $\rightarrow$ Spike $\rightarrow$ Sudden Slow-Motion Deceleration $\rightarrow$ Hero Hold**.
