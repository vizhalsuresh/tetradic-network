# AUXILIARY SHOW: THE SIGNAL — Production Guide

> **Premise**: Eccentric subjects, bizarre anomalies, and mathematical curiosities. Zero generic news vibe.  
> **Broadcast Slot**: Intermittent Bonus Drops

---

## 1. Show Narrative Arc (The Anomaly Dispatch)

1. **The Signal Intercept (0:00 - 0:45)**: Raw transmission incoming. An unexplainable event, undeciphered manuscript, or physics paradox.
2. **The Forensic Analysis (0:45 - 3:00)**: Code-drawn data forensics, spectral analysis, geometric decoders.
3. **The Leading Hypotheses (3:00 - 5:30)**: Competing theories tested and visualized.
4. **The Unresolved Horizon (5:30 - End)**: Leaving the viewer staring into the abyss of the unknown.

---

## 2. Visual Identity & Code Components

- **Aesthetic**: Deep void black, radar sweeps, phosphor CRT scanlines, cryptic data streams.
- **Palette Recommendation**: Phosphor amber, obsidian black, emerald radio glow.
- **Core Components**:
  - `RadarSweep`: Rotating azimuth sonar line revealing coordinate blips.
  - `CipherMatrix`: Cascading glyphs deciphering in real-time.
  - `OscilloscopeHUD`: Audio-reactive frequency waveforms.
