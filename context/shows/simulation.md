# SHOW 2: THE SIMULATION — Production Guide

> **Broadcast Slot**: Tuesday & Friday

## 1. Show Narrative Arc (The Stress-Test Method)

1. **The Hypothesis (0:00 - 0:30)**: State the impossible question (*"What happens to Earth if the Sun vanishes for 60 seconds?"*).
2. **The Baseline Model (0:30 - 2:00)**: Initialize the simulation parameters with clean, balanced physics.
3. **The Perturbation & Cascade (2:00 - 6:00)**: Introduce the shock event; watch failure cascade through swarms and equations.
4. **The Post-Mortem (6:00 - End)**: The simulated aftermath and fundamental physical truth.
