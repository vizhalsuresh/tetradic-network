# SHOW 3: THE PARADOX — Production Guide

> **Premise**: Explore counter-intuitive truths, cognitive glitches, game theory enigmas, and optical illusions that break intuition.  
> **Broadcast Slot**: Wednesday & Saturday

---

## 1. Show Narrative Arc (The Cognitive Glitch Method)

1. **The Trap (0:00 - 0:30)**: Present a problem where the viewer's common sense gives an immediate, confident, but 100% wrong answer.
2. **The Intuition Proof (0:30 - 2:00)**: Show why the human brain fails at this specific problem.
3. **The Geometric Reveal (2:00 - 5:00)**: Animate the mathematical truth using optical transformations and branching decision trees.
4. **The Reality Check (5:00 - End)**: Where this paradox silently operates in daily life, economics, or physics.

---

## 2. Visual Identity & Code Components

- **Aesthetic**: Monochromatic film noir, stark geometric contrast, optical illusions, dual-state geometry.
- **Palette Recommendation**: Sanzo Wada high-contrast duos (Deep Charcoal/Sumi with single piercing acid yellow or ultraviolet accent).
- **Core Components**:
  - `DecisionTree`: Branching probability paths that recalculate live.
  - `OpticalIllusion`: Shifting perspective lines demonstrating cognitive bias.
  - `DualStateMatrix`: Geometry that inverts when viewed under different conditions.
