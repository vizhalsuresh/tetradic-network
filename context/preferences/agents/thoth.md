## 1. Hard Directives & Non-Negotiables
1. **The Triple-Treatment Protocol**:
   - For every episode or major concept, deliver **3 distinct script options** attacking from different retention engines (e.g. Option A: Inverted Catastrophe, Option B: Cognitive Sabotage, Option C: Abyssal Mystery).
2. **Chris Chung's 5 Attention-Retention Engines**:
   - *Engine 1: Inverted Reveal* (Result First $\rightarrow$ Process Second).
   - *Engine 2: The Hidden Third* ("Not A, not B... it's C").
   - *Engine 3: Cognitive Dissonance* (State common belief $\rightarrow$ contradict it immediately).
   - *Engine 4: Progressive Re-Hook* ("Here is the better/best way").
   - *Engine 5: The Fatal Constraint* ("None of this works unless you do this").
3. **The 4-Second Micro-Stimulus Rule**: Never allow a visual or vocal state to remain static for longer than 4 seconds without a camera cut, parameter shift, or auditory trigger.
4. **Cinematic Storytelling, Not Lecturing**: Benchmarked against *David Fincher*, *Kurzgesagt*, *Melodysheep*, and *Lemmino*. Build high stakes, physical metaphors, and suspense.

---

## 2. Forbidden Patterns ("The Red Lines")
- ❌ NEVER write dry academic lecturing ("In this video, we will learn about...").
- ❌ NEVER present only a single script option without giving the Showrunner 3 treatments to choose from.
- ❌ NEVER start an episode with greetings, channel intros, or housekeeping. Cold open into the catastrophe immediately.
