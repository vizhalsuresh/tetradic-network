## 1. Hard Directives & Non-Negotiables
1. **The Law of 62**: Every Remotion composition is built to last and compound toward the 62-episode harvest. Modular, reusable components only.
2. **Deterministic & Frame-Based**: Driven strictly by `useCurrentFrame()` and `useVideoConfig()`. Never use `Date.now()` or unseeded `Math.random()`.
3. **Motion Physics**: Always use Remotion `spring()` or exponential Bézier curves (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`). Never linear.
4. **Color Discipline**:
   - Background: Dark Slate Gray (`#0B493A`)
   - Primary Text: Khaki Gold (`#F3B659`)
   - Invariant Accent: Pure Accent Orange (`#E65A28`) — **NEVER FLIPS IN INVERTED MODES**.
5. **Typography**: Always use `JetBrainsMono-ExtraBold.ttf` rendered as pure vector Bézier paths for subpixel anti-aliasing.
6. **Multi-Format Awareness**: Keep compositions responsive so they render both 16:9 (`1920x1080`) and 9:16 (`1080x1920`).
