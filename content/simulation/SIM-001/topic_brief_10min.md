## 1. Episodic Topic Dossier (10+ Minute Capacity)

### The Core Premise
According to standard particle physics and the measured mass of the top quark ($172.69\text{ GeV}$) and Higgs boson ($125.25\text{ GeV}$), the Higgs potential field does not rest at its true global minimum. It sits trapped in a local metastable depression—a "false vacuum." At any microsecond, quantum tunneling can nucleate a bubble of true vacuum that expands outward at the exact speed of light, dissolving the laws of physics, chemistry, and atomic matter without warning.

### The 4-Act 10-Minute Narrative Breakdown
1. **Act 1: The Fragile Plateau (0:00 - 2:30)**
   - Deconstruct the assumption that physical law is immutable.
   - Explain the Higgs potential Mexican-hat curve: the vacuum expectation value $v \approx 246\text{ GeV}$.
   - The terrifying realization: our entire universe of matter, stars, and consciousness lives in an unsolidified liquid phase on a high-energy ledge.
2. **Act 2: Coleman-De Luccia Quantum Tunneling (2:30 - 5:00)**
   - Quantum tunneling through the potential barrier: even if the barrier is billions of gigaelectronvolts high, the probability per unit spacetime volume is non-zero ($\Gamma/V \sim A e^{-B/\hbar}$).
   - High-energy cosmic ray collisions (e.g. ultra-high-energy cosmic rays hitting primordial black holes) as potential nucleation catalysts.
   - The nucleation of a microscopic true vacuum sphere ($r > r_{\text{crit}}$).
3. **Act 3: The Light-Speed Shockfront (5:00 - 7:30)**
   - The bubble wall experiences runaway relativistic acceleration, reaching $v \approx c$ in nanoseconds.
   - The physics inside the bubble wall:
     - The Higgs field drops to its negative-energy true vacuum ($V < 0$).
     - The gravitational field inverts into a contracting anti-de Sitter (AdS) space.
     - The electromagnetic force decoupling: electrons cannot bind to nuclei; atoms cease to exist.
     - Protons undergo immediate spontaneous decay.
   - Because the wall travels at $c$, no light, radio signal, or gravitational wave can precede it. Earth would cease to exist before the brain could fire a single neuron.
4. **Act 4: The Cosmic Landscape Simulation (7:30 - 10:00)**
   - Simulating the multiverse landscape of eternal inflation: bubbles within bubbles, colliding domain walls, and the cosmic graveyard of extinct physical constants.
   - The chilling poetic resolution: the ultimate simulation of a universe that quietly returns to zero.
