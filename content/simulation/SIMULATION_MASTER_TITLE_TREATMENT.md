## 1. Executive Narrative Thesis & The Core Cognitive Dissonance

### The Creative Thesis: *"Reality is a Model Under Stress Test"*
Most science and engineering channels treat natural law as eternal stone. *The Simulation* operates on a radically different psychological premise:

> **"Every physical law we trust—gravity, viscosity, thermodynamics, light speed—is merely a default parameter. When you dial that parameter to its breaking point, reality doesn't bend. It shatters."**

This is the psychological backbone of Show 2. Where Show 1 (*The Blueprint*) celebrates the triumph of human order and CAD drafting, *The Simulation* explores the terrifying awe of computational catastrophe, runaway cascades, and extreme physical limits.
