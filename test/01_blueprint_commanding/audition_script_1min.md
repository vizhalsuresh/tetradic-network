## The Script

> Look above your head. [pause 550ms]
>
> Twenty thousand kilometers into the vacuum of space, thirty-one atomic clocks are engaged in a silent war against the curvature of spacetime. [pause 500ms]
>
> Einstein's General Relativity dictates that clocks in weaker gravity tick forty-five microseconds faster every day. Special relativity pulls them back by seven microseconds. The net delta is thirty-eight point six microseconds per day. [pause 500ms]
>
> If our orbital engineers failed to hardcode that relativistic offset directly into the satellite oscillators before launch, every GPS coordinate on Earth would drift by eleven kilometers in twenty-four hours. [pause 650ms]
>
> And GPS is not navigation. It is the master heartbeat of human civilization. Wall Street automated trading, cellular packet handoffs, and national power grids are all slaved to that exact signal. A drift of four microseconds, and the modern financial architecture collapses into instant, unrecoverable chaos. [pause 750ms]
>
> This is the architecture of precision. [pause 500ms]
>
> This is the blueprint.
