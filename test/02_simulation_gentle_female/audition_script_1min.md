## The Script

> According to our best models of particle physics, the Higgs field that gives mass to the universe may not sit at its true minimum. We may be living inside a metastable false vacuum. A temporary bubble, waiting for a single quantum fluctuation. [pause 600ms]
>
> Somewhere in deep space, through pure quantum tunneling, a microscopic sphere of true vacuum could nucleate at this very second. Inside that bubble, the fundamental constants of nature will instantly invert. [pause 700ms]
>
> The bubble wall expands outward at the exact speed of light. Because it moves at the speed of light, no telescope can warn us. No sensor can see it coming. When it hits, atomic nuclei will simply dissolve. Electromagnetism will turn off. Every star, every planet, and every conscious mind will disintegrate without a sound. [pause 800ms]
>
> Let us run the simulation, and watch reality break apart.
