## The Script

> You believe you have a past. [pause 700ms]
>
> You believe you were born, that you grew up, that you are listening to these words right now. [pause 700ms]
>
> Thermodynamics says you are almost certainly wrong. [pause 850ms]
>
> The second law states that entropy must always increase. In an infinite, expanding cosmos, thermodynamic equilibrium is absolute coldness. But quantum mechanics allows random thermal fluctuations. [pause 650ms]
>
> Mathematically, it requires vastly less entropy for random quantum chaos to spontaneously fluctuate a single, isolated brain into existence in the dark void—imprinted with false memories of an entire universe—than it does to create an actual universe of two trillion galaxies. [pause 800ms]
>
> For every real observer that ever evolved, there are trillions upon trillions of Boltzmann brains hallucinating a reality that never existed. In a few seconds, the fluctuation will end, and you will dissolve back into the silence. [pause 900ms]
>
> Welcome to the paradox.
